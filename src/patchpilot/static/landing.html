SCOPE, WITHOUT GUESSWORK

A focused prototype.
A clear boundary.

Built around authorized, registered local Apache/PHP fixtures, with an owner in control of the repair.

Which websites can I assess?

Use a configured local URL from the dashboard's registered fleet. Other hosting environments require an authorized deployment connection and registration before assessment or repair.

What am I approving?

The specific assessments, proposed changes, required verification, and policy revision shown in the review. The backend binds that approval to the registered site scope.

How is a successful repair verified?

HTTP checks run on the staged candidate and again at the original address after activation. Reports retain observed runtime, functional checks, deployment identity, and recovery evidence.

What does the simulated walkthrough do?

It plays a clearly labeled example of assessment, repair progress, recipe reuse, and recovery. The simulated walkthrough does not operate the real local fixtures or provide real verification evidence.
