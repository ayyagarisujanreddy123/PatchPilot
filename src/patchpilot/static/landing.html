Which websites can I assess?

Public HTTP(S) pages on ports 80 or 443, without credentials or query parameters. Private and reserved destinations are excluded.

Will it change my website?

No. It collects public responses and suggests fixes. Applying a change requires your review and authorized access to your server or code.

Does a completed assessment mean my site is safe?

No. This is a public-response assessment, not a full security audit. Findings distinguish observed evidence, inference, and unknowns. Source and authenticated flows require further review.

Where is the repair lab?

The separate lab demonstrates controlled local PHP fixture repairs, with its own approval and verification workflow. Its simulation does not operate real websites.
